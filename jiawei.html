Research Interests

I am passionate about exploring biological, ecological, and evolutionary questions in diverse marine ecosystems. My research integrates multi-omics techniques with physiological and elemental measurements, aiming to connect cellular mechanisms with global biogeochemical processes. My current research interests include:

  • Seagrass–microbe interactions
  • Cycling and fluxes of the climate-cooling gas in seagrass meadows
